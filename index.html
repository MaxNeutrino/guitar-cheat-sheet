Circle of fourths & fifths

How to use the circle

    Symbols. Uppercase numerals (I IV V) are major chords, lowercase (ii iii vi) are minor, and ° means diminished: vii° in C is B° (B–D–F). An edge label such as 5♯ · 7♭ is one key with two spellings (B = C♭). Hover over any key to see its role in the selected key.

    Teal: current key & its diatonic window · Red: current chord · Arrow: last move · Select any key on the circle

    Chords in C major

    Shapes

    Outer ring: major keys. Inner ring: relative minors, which share the same key signature. Edge: sharps or flats in that signature. Seventh chords highlight their root on the major ring. Guitar shapes follow your tuning.
